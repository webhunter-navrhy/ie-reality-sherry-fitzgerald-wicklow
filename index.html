Description

This beautifully presented A-rated four bedroom semi-detached residence is ideally situated within the highly sought-after Haven Green development in Newtownmountkennedy, enjoying an enviable position overlooking an attractive open green space. Presented in turnkey condition and ready for immediate occupancy, this exceptional family home combines energy efficient modern living with stylish interior finishes throughout.

Extending to a generous and well-designed layout, the accommodation has been thoughtfully enhanced by the current owners and features elegant wall panelling, a bespoke fitted media unit and quality wood flooring throughout, creating a warm and contemporary atmosphere.

Upon entering, a welcoming hallway leads to a bright and spacious living room positioned to the front of the property, overlooking the green area. To the rear, the heart of the home is the impressive open-plan kitchen and dining area, offering an ideal space for both family living and entertaining. The contemporary kitchen is complemented by a practical utility room with additional storage and laundry facilities, while doors provide access to the sunny south-east facing rear garden.

Upstairs, the property offers four well-proportioned bedrooms, including a generous principal bedroom with ensuite bathroom. The remaining bedrooms are bright and versatile, making them ideal for family members, guests or a home office. A stylish family bathroom and guest WC complete the accommodation, providing a total of three bathrooms throughout the home.

Outside, the property benefits from private off-street parking to the front and a beautifully oriented south-east facing rear garden, perfect for enjoying morning and afternoon sunshine. The open green area to the front further enhances the sense of space and provides a safe and attractive setting for families.

Located within walking distance of schools, shops, sporting facilities and local amenities, Haven Green is a modern family-friendly development with excellent access to the N11/M11, making commuting to Dublin, Wicklow Town and beyond both quick and convenient.

Combining a superb location, contemporary styling, excellent energy efficiency and turnkey presentation, this outstanding home offers a wonderful opportunity for those seeking a high-quality family residence in the heart of Newtownmountkennedy.
